FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to open an account? ▼
Account opening takes 15–30 minutes online. After submitting your application, our RM will call within 2 hours for Video KYC. Once KYC is verified, your account is typically activated within 24 hours.
What documents do I need? ▼
You need: (1) PAN Card, (2) Aadhaar Card (front & back), (3) Passport size photo, (4) Bank statement/cancelled cheque, and optionally your signature on white paper. All can be uploaded digitally.
What is the minimum amount needed to start trading? ▼
There is no minimum amount to open an account. For equity delivery, you can start with as little as ₹500. For F&O trading, you need sufficient margin as required by the exchange (typically ₹50,000–₹1,00,000).
How are margins calculated? ▼
Margins are calculated per SEBI guidelines using SPAN (Standard Portfolio Analysis of Risk) + Exposure margin for F&O trades. For intraday equity, we offer up to 5x leverage. All margin requirements are shown before order placement.
What happens if my margin falls below required levels? ▼
You'll receive an instant WhatsApp alert when your margin utilization crosses 80%. If it crosses 90%, our system may initiate auto square-off of positions to protect you from further losses. You can add funds anytime to avoid this.
How do I receive my contract notes? ▼
Contract notes are generated automatically after market hours and sent to your registered email and WhatsApp. They include all trade details, charges (brokerage, STT, GST, exchange charges), and net obligation for settlement.
Is my money safe with Bizaxl? ▼
Yes. Bizaxl Securities is SEBI registered. Client funds are held in separate accounts per SEBI guidelines. Your securities are held in your own CDSL/NSDL demat account, not ours. We cannot access your funds without explicit authorization.
